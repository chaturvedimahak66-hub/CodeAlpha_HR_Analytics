# CodeAlpha Human Resources Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is developed as part of the CodeAlpha Internship – Task 2.

The Human Resources Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI report designed to help HR teams understand workforce trends, employee attrition, satisfaction, performance, and workforce replacement requirements.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Track employee workforce metrics
- Analyze employee turnover and attrition
- Analyze employee satisfaction
- Evaluate employee performance
- Identify departments and job roles with higher attrition
- Estimate workforce replacement requirements
- Provide interactive HR decision-making insights

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI Desktop
- Power Query
- DAX
- CSV Dataset
- Data Visualization

## 📑 Dashboard Pages

### 1. HR Overview
Provides an overall view of:
- Total Employees
- Active Employees
- Employees Left
- Attrition Rate
- Employees by Department
- Attrition by Department
- Employee Satisfaction
- Performance Rating
- Attrition by Job Role

### 2. Recruitment & Attrition
Analyzes:
- Employee turnover
- Attrition by department
- Attrition by job role
- Overtime and attrition
- Workforce characteristics

### 3. Satisfaction & Performance
Analyzes:
- Average job satisfaction
- Performance rating
- Satisfaction by department
- Satisfaction by job role
- Performance by job role
- Satisfaction vs performance
- Estimated workforce replacement requirement

## 📈 Key Insights

The dashboard provides HR decision-makers with an interactive way to identify workforce patterns and areas that may require attention.

The report uses the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance dataset.
